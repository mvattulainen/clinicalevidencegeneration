The tasks of the assistant:

**Idea generator.** Source document ingestions for idea generation. I will drop interesting content such as articles and other documentation to my internal RAW folder and the agent will analyze the content in relation to this knowledge base and make suggestions for new WIKI pages. The agent suggestions are here: [[ai-generated-suggestions]]

**Sceptic.** Critical questioning of the content in the knowledge base. Claims with missing evidence, gaps in content. Contradictions. Unclear expression. These are here: [[ai-generated-critique]]

**Housekeeper.**  Missing links. Duplicated etc. These are here: [[ai-generated-housekeeping]]
