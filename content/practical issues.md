
In addition to the exclusive focus on regulatory approval and neglect of the commercial application domain of clinical evidence here are some of the other issues based on author's experiences with mostly start-up medical device manufacturers.

Root cause: Clinical evaluation is perceived as bureaucratic necessity with little or no commercial impact.

Impact 1: Clinical evaluation is done too late as part of the CE-marking submission packages. This poor timing often reveals new competitors, new features in competitor's products and general safety observations that should have been part of design input right from the beginning.

Impact 2: The clinical benefits claims studied and ever demonstrated in a clinical investigation are not related to sales arguments.

Impact 3: The manufacturer may have barely any real connection to clinical practices and therefore fails to understand the actual clinical use of the product.
