
Clinical data strategy identifies the users of the evidence and their expectations.

Users include:
- customers using the device
- customers paying for the use of the device
- sales/ distribution channels
- reimbursement providers
- clinical guidance makers
- regulatory bodies

> [!Accumulation]
> The essence of clinical data strategy is accumulation. Achieving accumulation requires that 1) the clinical benefit claims are or at least their change is planned, 2) there is consistency of end-points from study to study.

The expectations vary. Here are some:
- A regulatory body such as EU notified body requires ISO 14155 compliant clinical investigation for class III device unless equivalence is demonstrated. 
- A clinician may consider sponsor-led investigations inferior and biased compared to independent studies on the device
